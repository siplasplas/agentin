# Contributing to agentin

Thank you for helping with agentin. Before you send a change, please read the
rules below; by contributing you accept them.

## License of contributions

agentin is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 only
(GPL-3.0-only); see `LICENSE`. Your contribution is licensed under the same
terms as the rest of agentin.

## Later versions of the GPL

Section 14 of the GPL-3.0 allows a program to name a proxy who can decide which
future versions of the GNU General Public License may be used. For agentin, the
proxy is its author, Andrzej Borucki, or a person he names publicly as the
proxy. When a later version of the GPL is published, the proxy may accept it by
a public statement, such as a note in this repository; from then on, agentin,
including your contribution, may be used under that version as well.

By contributing, you agree that this proxy decision applies to your
contribution, so that agentin never needs the consent of every contributor to
move to a later version of the GPL.

## How to contribute

- Write code, comments, documentation and commit messages in English.
- Match the style of the surrounding code; the rules for coding assistants in
  `AGENTS.md` describe the project's conventions as well.
- Describe user-visible changes in `README.md`.
- Keep private data out of commits: keys, tokens, personal paths, e-mail
  addresses, logs and settings files.
